Our Mission

Bridging Erode MSMEs & Campus Talent

Empowering traditional businesses with modern digital capabilities while providing students with genuine paid freelance experience.

The Problem in Erode

Erode is widely renowned as the textile and turmeric hub of Tamil Nadu, home to thousands of powerloom units, textile wholesale traders, turmeric merchants, retail shops, and engineering fabricators. However, most small and micro-businesses struggle with digital adoption — high agency retainers are unaffordable for simple tasks like setting up Google Maps, creating social media banners, automating Excel spreadsheets, or launching a 5-page fabric catalog website.

At the same time, thousands of bright undergraduate students across Kongu Engineering College, Bannari Amman Institute of Technology, IRTT, Erode Arts and Science College, and other institutions seek real-world portfolio experience and productive gig earnings outside classroom theory.

Our Solution: GigCampus

GigCampus is a dedicated hyperlocal marketplace specifically created to bridge this local divide. By verifying business registrations and linking student credentials with recognized institutions, we build a trust-first environment where small business owners can post discrete tasks and students can deliver quality work directly.
