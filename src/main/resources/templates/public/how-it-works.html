How GigCampus Operates

From registration to final project completion, here is how businesses and students connect seamlessly.

1. Onboarding

Students and business owners register with verified emails and location details in Erode.

2. Gig Posting

Businesses specify required digital tasks (websites, spreadsheets, banners, SEO) and set their budget.

3. Proposal Review

Students submit custom proposals with delivery timelines. The business reviews and selects a candidate.

4. Delivery & Review

Work is delivered, project is marked completed, and authentic reviews build student credentials.
