Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

GigCampus collects account information such as student full name, email, phone number, college, department, and portfolio links. For business accounts, we collect business name, trade category, location within Erode, and optional registration numbers (such as GSTIN) submitted for verification.

2. Password Security

We store passwords using industry-standard one-way cryptographic salted hashes (BCrypt). Plaintext passwords are never logged, stored, or visible to system administrators.

3. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

Student application details and contact information are disclosed solely to the specific business whose gig they applied for. We do not sell or monetize personal student or business data to third-party ad networks.

4. Business Verification Documents

Any shop registration details or GSTIN data uploaded for verification purposes are restricted to authorized GigCampus administrators for review and trust checks.
