Frequently Asked Questions

Any enrolled student at a recognized college, polytechnic, or university in and around Erode (e.g. Kongu Engineering, BIT Sathyamangalam, IRTT, Erode Arts & Science, Vellalar, etc.) can register, build a profile, and apply for open gigs.

The proposed and agreed budgets are recorded within GigCampus to maintain clear milestone expectations. Actual financial settlements (via UPI, bank transfer, or cash on delivery) are handled directly between the business owner and the student. GigCampus does not hold funds or store credit card details in this initial release.

Businesses register with initial UNVERIFIED status. They can submit their GSTIN or shop establishment details from their profile. GigCampus administrators review these details before awarding a "Verified" badge to safeguard students.
